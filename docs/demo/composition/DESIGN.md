# SILICONPILOT film identity

## Style Prompt
A scientific hardware observatory printed on warm archival paper: large Georgia statements, quiet system sans interface labels, exact monospaced measurement readouts, dark green ink and a single vermilion path. Favor diagrams and genuine screenshots over decorative motion. The composition extends the existing observatory's identity.

## Colors
- #f2f0e9 paper
- #fbfaf6 surface
- #202927 ink
- #d94a25 design frontier and active signal
- #286b50 verified evidence

## Typography
Georgia for statements. Arial/system sans for product copy. SFMono-Regular/Menlo monospace for technical measurements. Existing UI screenshots preserve their native typography.

## Motion
0.45-second crossfades, short 0.5–0.8-second entrances, long readable holds. Deterministic slow screenshot camera movement. No decorative particles, gradients, bloom, bouncing counters or invented progress.
